About Euphoria 2024

Welcome to *Euphoria 2024*, an annual arts fest organized by the student union MBSA at UP USTHAD MEMORIAL DAWA COLLEGE. Our fest, themed "The Arts of Living," celebrates the vibrant world of arts and culture through a diverse range of performances, exhibitions, and workshops.

This year's fest promises to be a remarkable experience with participants showcasing their talents across various categories including music, dance, drama, and visual arts. Our aim is to provide a platform for artistic expression and to foster a sense of community and creativity among students and guests.

Purpose

The primary objective of *Euphoria 2024* is to create a dynamic space where students and artists can explore and present their artistic talents. By facilitating various events and competitions, we aim to inspire creativity, encourage collaboration, and promote the importance of the arts in education and everyday life.

We believe that art has the power to transform lives and build stronger communities. Through this fest, we hope to ignite a passion for the arts and contribute to the cultural enrichment of our college and beyond.

Organizers

*Euphoria 2024* is proudly organized by the student union MBSA at UP USTHAD MEMORIAL DAWA COLLEGE. Our dedicated team of students has worked tirelessly to bring this event to life, ensuring a memorable and impactful experience for all attendees.

We would like to extend our heartfelt gratitude to all the volunteers, sponsors, and participants who have supported us in making this fest a success. Your enthusiasm and commitment are what make *Euphoria 2024* a celebration of art and creativity.
